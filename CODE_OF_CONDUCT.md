# Code of conduct

Easy Review is a study project. People come here while preparing for exams, often on their own time, and contributors range from first-time learners to people who hold every AWS Certification. Treat everyone the way you would want to be treated when you are the one who doesn't know the answer yet.

## Expected

- Be patient and kind, in issues, pull requests and reviews alike.
- Criticize the fact, the code or the idea, never the person.
- Back a correction with the AWS documentation page that shows it.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing exam content under NDA, or copied course or practice-exam material.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@jhermesn](https://github.com/jhermesn)) may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/jhermesn/easyreview/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies in this repository and wherever someone represents the project. For a fuller treatment of these ideas, see the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
