---
title: IAM
names: ["AWS Identity and Access Management", "IAM", "AWS Security Token Service", "AWS STS"]
summary: Control AWS access with identities, temporary role credentials, policy grants and permission limits.
icon: identity-and-access-management
---

IAM policies decide which identities may perform actions on AWS resources. Roles provide temporary access, while trust policies, resource policies and permission limits determine how that access is granted.
