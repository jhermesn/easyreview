---
title: Application Recovery Controller
names: ["Amazon Application Recovery Controller", "Route 53 Application Recovery Controller", "Application Recovery Controller"]
summary: Move traffic away from an impaired zone or switch DNS traffic between regional application
  replicas.
icon: application-recovery-controller
---

Amazon Application Recovery Controller (ARC) supports recovery through zonal shifts and regional routing controls. Zonal shifts avoid one Availability Zone; routing controls change Route 53 health checks to switch traffic between application replicas.
