AWS Service Catalog offers a menu of approved AWS products that users can launch. [What is Service Catalog?](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/servicecatalog/latest/adminguide/introduction.html#introduction)

### How it works

1. Administrators add approved products to a portfolio.
2. Users get access to the portfolio.
3. Users launch products from the catalog.

### Exam cues

- If the question says let users launch only approved products, think Service Catalog.

### Remember

- Catalogs of approved IT services.

> **Trap:** Service Catalog controls what users can launch; it is not a marketplace for buying software.
